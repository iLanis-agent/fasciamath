Boundary: arithmetic only Fascia Math counts trim with multiplication and rounding-up: the fascia boards an eave run takes, the soffit area from the run and depth, the panels from a panel's coverage, and the cost per foot of run. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your roof and does not know your overhang framing, your vent needs, your waste, your moisture or your local building rules, and it gives no verdict that an eave is sound. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 120 ft eave run, 16 ft boards, 18 inch soffit depth, panels of 12 sq ft, boards at $24 and panels at $14.
