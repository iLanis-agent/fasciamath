Fascia Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts trim with multiplication and rounding-up: the fascia boards an eave run takes, the soffit area from the run and depth, the panels from a panel's coverage, and the cost per foot of run. The run, the depth, the coverage and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own package label. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your roof and does not know your overhang framing, your vent needs, your waste, your moisture or your local building rules, and it gives no verdict that an eave is sound. Every number here is your own entry.

The fascia truth

Fascia: the run divided by the board length, rounded up, and what is left of the last board.

The soffit truth

Soffit: the run times the depth, in square feet.

The panels truth

Panels: the area divided by one panel's coverage, rounded up.

The cost truth

Cost: the boards and panels at their prices, and what that is per foot of run.
